About Triage AI

We're building the future of autonomous intelligence — where users own their agents, their data, and their decisions.

Our Mission

Triage AI exists to dismantle the assumption that powerful AI requires surrendering your privacy. We architect zero-knowledge agent systems that execute complex workflows — research, orchestration, deployment — without ever seeing, storing, or monetizing your data.

Founded in Pueblo, Colorado, Triage AI LLC is a lean, founder-led company laser-focused on giving individuals, enterprises, and governments AI tools they can actually trust.

🎯

Zero-Knowledge by Default

Every architectural decision starts with one question: "Does the user retain full control?" If the answer is no, we redesign it.
