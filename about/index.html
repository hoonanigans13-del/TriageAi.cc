About Triage AI

Triage AI was built on a simple conviction: the public deserves to know how government systems use their data — and that knowledge requires infrastructure, not just policy.

The Civic Intelligence Ledger is the operational expression of that conviction. It is not a watchdog organization. It is not an advocacy group. It is infrastructure — the kind of infrastructure that makes watchdogs, journalists, oversight bodies, and informed citizens possible.

Government data systems are large, distributed, and often opaque. Data enters them through dozens of channels, passes through many hands, and exits — sometimes to contractors, sometimes to other agencies, sometimes to parties that were never disclosed at the point of collection. Current disclosure regimes are reactive, incomplete, and difficult to audit at scale.

Triage AI changes that by making the audit layer continuous, machine-readable, and tamper-evident. Not after the fact. Not on request. Continuously.
