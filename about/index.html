About Triage AI

AI that works for you.

Triage AI LLC is an independent software company based in Pueblo, CO. We build zero-knowledge, user-owned AI systems — autonomous intelligence that works for the person running it, not the company that made it.

What we believe

Our principles.

🔒

Your data is yours

We design every feature with one constraint: your data never leaves your device. No analytics. No training data harvesting. No exceptions.

⚙️

Autonomy is earned

Triage doesn't demand trust — it earns it through a verifiable, local trust score built from your own decisions. You control when and how much autonomy it has.

🪟

Transparent by design

Every action Triage takes in autonomous mode is logged in a local audit trail you can inspect at any time. No black boxes. No hidden behavior.

🚫

No account required

Your license is a signed file on your device. There is no Triage account to create, breach, or subpoena. We genuinely can't see you.

Why we built Triage.
