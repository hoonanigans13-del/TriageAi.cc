Android install guide

Installing the APK.

Because this is a direct APK (not from the Play Store), you need to allow installs from unknown sources once. Here's how:

01

Download the APK

Tap "Download APK" above. Your browser will save TriageAI-v1.0.0.apk to your Downloads folder.

02

Allow installs from this source

When prompted, tap Settings and enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager. This is a one-time step.

03

Open the APK file

Open your Downloads folder, tap TriageAI-v1.0.0.apk, and tap Install.

04

Launch Triage

Open Triage from your app drawer. Your encrypted vault is created on first launch — no account, no sign-up.
