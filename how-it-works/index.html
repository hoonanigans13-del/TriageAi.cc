How Triage works

Intelligence that earns its autonomy.

Triage doesn't ask for permission up front. It starts in suggestion-only mode, learns your priorities, and builds a trust score based on how well its suggestions match your decisions. Once it hits 97 % — you unlock autonomous mode.

Step by step

From install to autonomous.

01

Install & set up your vault

Triage creates an AES-256 encrypted vault on your device during first launch. No account, no cloud setup — just a local key only you hold. Your data never leaves this vault.

02

Connect your inbox

Link your email account via a local OAuth flow. Triage reads and processes messages entirely on-device. No email content is ever sent to a server — not even ours.

03

Triage suggests — you decide

In the beginning, Triage surfaces prioritized suggestions: reply to this, archive that, flag this as urgent. You act on them (or don't), and Triage records your decisions locally to refine its model.

04

Trust score climbs

Every accepted suggestion increases your trust score. Every override teaches Triage something new. The score is computed locally — no telemetry, no profile building on any server.

05

Autonomous mode unlocks at 97 %

At 97 % trust, Triage starts acting on your behalf: scheduling, archiving, drafting replies. Every action is logged in a local audit trail you can inspect, replay, or revoke at any time.

Connection Guide

Linking your email locally.

Because Triage is zero-knowledge, it connects directly from your device to your email provider. It does not use "Login with Google" because that would involve our servers. Follow this guide to set up your private local connection.

1. What is an IMAP Server?

This is the "address" of your email provider. Enter the following in the IMAP Server field in Triage:

Provider Server Address
Gmail imap.gmail.com
Outlook/Hotmail outlook.office365.com
iCloud imap.mail.me.com
Yahoo imap.mail.yahoo.com

2. Use an App Password

For your security, providers like Google and Microsoft block your regular password for local apps. You must generate a one-time App Password.

  • 🔗 Setup Gmail App Password
  • 🔗 Setup Outlook App Password
  • 🔗 Setup iCloud App Password

Security Note: App Passwords can be revoked at any time and never reveal your main password to the application.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does Triage ever send my emails to a server?

Never. All email processing happens on-device inside your encrypted vault. The only network call Triage makes is the initial OAuth handshake with your email provider — and that goes directly to your provider, not to us.

What happens if I uninstall Triage?

Your vault is deleted along with the app. There is nothing stored server-side to retrieve or delete — because nothing was ever sent there.

Can I reset my trust score?

Yes. You can reset the trust score from settings at any time, which returns Triage to suggestion-only mode. Your vault data is preserved.

What does autonomous mode actually do?

It allows Triage to take pre-approved actions — archiving low-priority mail, sending pre-approved reply templates, scheduling events — without waiting for your tap. Every action is logged locally.
