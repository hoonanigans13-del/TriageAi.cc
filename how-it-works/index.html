HOW TRIAGE WORKS

Intelligence that earns its trust.

Triage operates 100% on your device. Choose between two distinct operating modes based on your workflow needs.

STREAMLINED ASSISTANT

Everyday Assistant Mode

Built for zero clutter and effortless everyday inbox triage.

  1. Sign in with your email & password.
  2. Enable quick biometric unlock (Fingerprint, Touch ID, or Face ID).
  3. Triage automatically organizes incoming notifications and emails locally.
  4. Accept or dismiss suggestions in one tap — no clutter, no raw technical menus.
SOVEREIGN VAULT

Sovereign Secure Edition

Built for maximum privacy, custom rules, and local master vault protection.

  1. Connect directly to your IMAP mail server (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo).
  2. Authenticate with a 6-digit 2FA Authenticator TOTP code.
  3. Set a Master Vault Passphrase to encrypt local data with AES-256-GCM.
  4. Access complete bylaws, trust audit history, and offline Ed25519 verification.
