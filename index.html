How Triage works.

1

Install & Unlock

Download Triage for Windows, Android, Linux, or iOS. Create your encrypted vault with a passphrase — no account, no email.

2

Connect Your Inbox

Link your email in the app. All processing happens locally on your device. Nothing leaves.

3

Triage Learns Your Priorities
