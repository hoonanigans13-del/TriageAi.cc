How Triage Works

Meet Your Neural Apprentice

Triage is a privacy-first AI companion designed to reduce cognitive load around digital communication. Unlike standard automation tools, Triage earns your trust before it ever acts on your behalf — learning your habits, preferences, and priorities through observation before taking any autonomous steps.

👁

Observation Mode

Triage starts by watching — learning how you handle emails, schedule meetings, and manage communications, without touching a thing.

🧠

High-Context Assistance

As it learns, Triage offers smart suggestions: drafting replies, flagging schedule conflicts, and organizing attachments from trusted contacts.

🔒

Trust-Based Learning

Your communication patterns stay yours. Triage builds a trust model around your verified contacts and habits — never sharing your data externally.

🤝

Autonomous Partnership

Only after reaching verified precision can you choose to unlock Autonomous Partnership — letting Triage act on your behalf, on your terms.

97%

The Precision Threshold

Triage operates on a 97% precision standard. Only after the AI reaches this level of verified, demonstrated trust does it offer you the option to authorize Autonomous Partnership — giving you full confidence before any action is ever taken without your direct input.
