How Triage works

Intelligence that earns its autonomy.

Triage starts in suggestion-only mode. As it learns your preferences it builds trust, and once it reaches 97 % you unlock autonomous mode. Everything stays on your device the whole time.

🔒

Local encrypted vault

Every email, task, and decision Triage touches is stored in an AES-256 vault on your device. Only you hold the key.

📊

Trust meter

Triage earns autonomy incrementally. It starts by suggesting. At 97 % trust, autonomous mode unlocks — on your terms.

📬

Email intelligence

Triage reads, prioritizes, and acts on your inbox based on rules you control. No third-party access. No cloud processing.

⚡

Autonomous mode

At 97 % trust, Triage schedules, responds, and triages on your behalf — with a full local audit log you can inspect anytime.

Zero-knowledge design

No account. No cloud. No exceptions.

Triage never phones home. Your vault is yours. Your license is a signed file on your device. There is no Triage account to create, breach, or subpoena.
