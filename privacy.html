Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Triage AI (“Triage AI”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our services, including our website, applications, and integrations with third‑party providers such as Google. Triage AI is designed around zero‑knowledge and user‑controlled data principles: we aim to minimize what we see, what we store, and how long we keep it.

1. Who we are

Triage AI is a privacy‑first assistant and workflow system that helps you triage information and tasks across your tools. Our goal is to give you useful automation while keeping you in control of your data.

2. What information we collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve the service.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account or contact information: If you contact us or sign up for updates, we may collect your name, email address, and any message content you send.
  • Content you submit: Text you type into Triage AI (for example, prompts, notes, or configuration) may be processed to respond to your requests and operate your workflows.

2.2 Usage information

  • Log data: Basic technical logs (such as timestamps, error messages, and performance metrics) may be collected to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Device and connection data: Limited information such as browser type, operating system, and general region may be processed to support security and debugging.

2.3 Third‑party integrations

If you choose to connect Triage AI to third‑party services (such as Google), we may receive limited data from those services strictly as authorized by you and by the scopes you approve during the consent process.

3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the service: Process your requests, run workflows, and deliver responses.
  • To maintain security and reliability: Detect, prevent, and address errors, abuse, or technical issues.
  • To improve the service: In some cases, aggregated or de‑identified data may be used to understand performance and reliability trends.
  • To communicate with you: Respond to support requests or send important service‑related notices.

4. Data storage and retention

  • Minimal retention: We aim to store only what is necessary to operate the service and only for as long as needed.
  • Configuration and logs: Some configuration data and system logs may be retained for security, debugging, and audit purposes.
  • Deletion: If you request deletion of specific data that we control, we will make reasonable efforts to delete it, except where we are required to retain it by law or for security purposes.

5. Google integrations and API data

Triage AI may integrate with Google services (such as Google APIs) to perform actions you explicitly request. When you connect a Google account, we access only the data necessary to fulfill the features you choose to use.

5.1 Google user data we may access

Depending on the scopes you approve, Triage AI may access limited Google data such as:

  • Basic profile information (for identification and account linking).
  • Specific resources required for a given workflow (for example, reading or modifying particular items you explicitly select, if such features are enabled).

The exact data accessed is always constrained by the OAuth scopes you see and approve in Google’s consent screen.

5.2 How we use Google user data

  • To perform actions you request: For example, if you ask Triage AI to read or modify certain data in a connected Google service, we use the authorized access solely to carry out that instruction.
  • To maintain your connection: We may store tokens necessary to keep the integration working, subject to Google’s policies and your revocation options.

5.3 No selling or unrelated use of Google data

Triage AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not use Google user data to build profiles for advertising.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties except as necessary to provide the service, comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.

5.4 Limited Use compliance

Where Triage AI accesses data via Google APIs that is covered by the Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • Data is used only to provide or improve user‑facing features that are prominent in the Triage AI experience.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve those features, comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.
  • Data is not used for serving ads, profiling for ads, or other unrelated purposes.
  • Any stored data is protected with reasonable and appropriate security measures.

5.5 Revoking access to Google data

You can revoke Triage AI’s access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account’s security settings and removing the Triage AI integration, or by adjusting permissions in the relevant Google service.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers: With trusted infrastructure or service providers who help us operate the service (for example, hosting, logging, or monitoring), under contractual obligations to protect your data.
  • Legal and safety: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Triage AI, our users, or others.
  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share specific information for a specific purpose.

7. Security

We use reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and you should use strong security practices on your own devices and accounts.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Third‑party connections: You can choose whether or not to connect Triage AI to services like Google, and you can revoke access at any time.
  • Data access and deletion: If you would like to request access to or deletion of data that Triage AI controls about you, you can contact us using the information below.
  • Configuration: You can adjust how you use Triage AI to limit what information you send through it.

9. Children’s privacy

Triage AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International use

Triage AI may be operated from servers located in the United States or other jurisdictions. By using the service, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in these locations, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice (for example, via the website or by email, where appropriate). Your continued use of Triage AI after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
