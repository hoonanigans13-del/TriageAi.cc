Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Triage is a privacy‑first, zero‑knowledge AI assistant designed so your data stays under your control. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Triage is the product name. The service is operated by Triage AI LLC, based in Pueblo, Colorado. Contact us at support@triageai.cc.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Content you submit to Triage
  • Contact information if you reach out for support
  • Optional settings or preferences you configure

Automatically collected information

  • Basic device information (browser type, operating system, screen size)
  • Non‑identifying usage logs (page loads, errors)

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not store your messages.
  • We do not store your emails.
  • We do not store your tasks, notes, or personal content beyond your active session.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

3. Google User Data — OAuth Disclosure

Triage uses Google OAuth 2.0 to allow users to connect their Google account. When you sign in with Google or authorize Triage to access Google services, we may receive and use the following data:

Data accessed from Google

  • Email address, name, and profile picture — received at sign-in via Google's OpenID Connect (scopes: openid, profile, email). Used solely for account identification and personalization within Triage.
  • Gmail data (if authorized) — Triage may read email metadata and message content to help you manage, triage, and draft replies to your communications. This data is processed in real time and is not stored on Triage servers beyond your active session.
  • Google Calendar data (if authorized) — Triage may read and suggest changes to your calendar events to help identify scheduling conflicts and organize your time. Calendar data is processed in real time and not persistently stored.

How we use Google data

  • To authenticate you and identify your account
  • To provide the core Triage experience: email triage, drafting, and scheduling assistance
  • To display your data back to you within the Triage interface

How we do NOT use Google data

  • We do not use Google user data to train AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not retain Google user data on our servers after your session ends, except where you explicitly save a preference or setting.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Triage's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

We use the minimal information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the service
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal requirements

We do not use your data to train external AI models.

5. When we share information

Service providers

We may share information with trusted infrastructure or service providers who help us operate the service (for example, hosting, logging, or monitoring). These providers are under contractual obligations to protect your data.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Triage AI LLC, our users, or others.

With your consent

We may share information when you explicitly authorize us to share specific information for a specific purpose.

6. Data security

We use industry‑standard encryption and zero‑knowledge design principles. Your data is processed locally or through user‑controlled infrastructure whenever possible.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use Triage without creating an account (where applicable)
  • Revoke Google account access at any time via your Google Account permissions
  • Delete your Triage data by contacting support@triageai.cc
  • Control what Triage can access or automate

8. Children's privacy

Triage is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
