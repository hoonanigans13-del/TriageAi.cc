Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Triage AI operates triageai.cc and the Civic Intelligence Ledger platform. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and what rights you have over it. We collect the minimum necessary. We do not sell data. We do not run behavioral advertising.

What We Collect

When you visit triageai.cc, our hosting infrastructure automatically logs standard server request data: IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps. This is retained for security and operational purposes only.

If you contact us by email, we retain that correspondence to respond to your inquiry and maintain a record of the communication.

We do not use tracking cookies, behavioral analytics, or third-party advertising pixels.

How We Use It

  • To operate and improve the site
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We may share information with service providers who assist with site operation (hosting, security), under contracts that prohibit them from using the data for any other purpose. We will disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Retention

Server logs are retained for 90 days and then deleted. Email correspondence is retained for as long as the matter remains active and for a reasonable period afterward. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.
